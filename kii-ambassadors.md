# Kii Ambassadors

### What does it mean to become a Kii Ambassador?

Welcome to the **Kii Ambassadors Program** — our exclusive initiative to recognize and reward the most dedicated voices in the KiiChain community.

Being an ambassador is more than just a title — it’s a position of influence and responsibility for those who share our vision and want to help grow the project, both on-chain and online.

_All information, tasks, and rewards are shared in the **Private Ambassadors Channel** on our official Discord._

You can apply to join via the official KiiChain Discord (Official Links Channel): [https://discord.gg/kiichain](https://discord.gg/kiichain)

#### Kii Ambassadors are selected community members who:

* Amplify KiiChain’s messages on X (Twitter) and other platforms.
* Stay active and helpful in Discord.
* Bring new users to the chain and nurture community spirit.
* Represent the KiiChain brand publicly, respectfully, and consistently.

---

### Requirements to Join

To be accepted into the program, you must:

* Have a solid follower base and good engagement on social media.
* Follow KiiChain on X.
* Be registered on the ORO Testnet.

---

### Core Responsibilities

To maintain your role, you are expected to:

* Engage weekly in the Kii Discord, especially in public channels.
* React, share, and comment on KiiChain’s official posts on X.
* Participate in weekly tasks shared in the Ambassadors Channel.
* Help grow the community by inviting new users or explaining Kii to newcomers.
* Recommend strong candidates for the program.

---

### Participation Perks

Ambassadors who complete tasks consistently and with high quality may receive:

* Rewards in $ORO and $KII.
* Monthly raffles for top ambassadors with USDT prizes.
* Shoutouts on official socials and Discord.
* Access to exclusive giveaways.
* Special Q&A sessions with the KiiChain Core Team.
* Early access to new features, tools, and campaigns.

---

### Points System & Performance

To ensure fairness and reward real contributions, we use a **Points System** to track all ambassador activities.  
Each action — such as retweeting, commenting, creating content, participating in Discord, or attending events — earns points.

At the end of each month, top-performing ambassadors are rewarded based on their accumulated points.

**The mod team will share a weekly form where you can submit proof of your activities to ensure accurate point allocation.**

### Rewards

#### Monthly Reward

* **2,000 USDT in rewards for the top contributors of the month.**
* Every active ambassador is eligible to receive $300 USD worth of $KII per month, subject to:
  * At least 1 original post per week (thread, infographic, or video).
  * Engagement (like, retweet, comment) on every official Kii post shared in the ambassador group.
  * At least 3 helpful messages per week in public Discord channels.
  * Completion and submission of weekly tasks via our tracking system.

_**Note:** Rewards are vested with a 3-month cliff followed by 18-month linear vesting, based on a $100M FDV for $KII._

---

### Ambassador Incentives & Referral-Based Role System

The program rewards not only consistency and engagement, but also your ability to expand the community with quality contributors.

For every 3 new **Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs)** you refer who are accepted into the program:

* Your role is upgraded.
* You receive a $200 USD bonus in $KII (vesting schedule applies).

Ambassadors must also publish at least 4 pieces of original content per month to remain active.

### Referral Qualification

For a referral to count:

* The referred KOL must follow KiiChain on X.
* They must join and be approved in the official Ambassador Discord.
* The referral must be reported and verified by the Kii team.

---

### Grounds for Removal

You may lose your role if you:

* Spread FUD or attack the KiiChain project/team.
* Share scam links or break Discord rules.
* Leak confidential or early information.
* Remain inactive without justification for a prolonged period.
* Consistently skip or ignore assigned tasks.

---

### Evaluation Process

Every 30 days, the KiiChain team will evaluate ambassadors based on:

* Task completion.
* Quality of participation.
* Alignment with community values.

Those not meeting the standards may be removed to make space for new voices.
